Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only place we know of with life. From space it looks mostly blue, because water covers about 71% of its surface.

Earth is about 4.5 billion years old. It spins once a day, circles the Sun once a year, and carries about 8 billion people along for the ride.

Three facts about Earth

  • One trip around the Sun takes 365.25 days. That extra quarter day is why we have leap years.
  • The deepest known point in the ocean is about 11 kilometers down.
  • The Moon drifts away from Earth about 4 centimeters every year.
